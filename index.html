Nearby Industrial Hub

Axis Industrial Park · ITC

A 50-acre Rajasthan government-approved industrial township on Alwar–Bhiwadi State Highway 25, about 2 km from Aqasia Homes. With ITC purchasing 30 acres for its Aashirvaad Atta manufacturing unit, the region is set for increased employment and strong land-value growth.

Leading Brands Nearby

Honda, Hindware, Kajaria, P&G, Pepsi, Jaguar Group, Relaxo, Kerovit, UB, Parryware, Dr. Oetker, Hero, Saint-Gobain, and Membrane Group form a strong industrial ecosystem around Bhiwadi.

Cargo Airport · NCR’s 3rd at Bhiwadi

Upcoming airport at Kotkasim Village, Alwar district, under the Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC). A catalyst for trading across Bhiwadi, Rewari, Neemrana, and Manesar, building a major industrial hub in the region.
